Everyday use

You just talk. There is nothing to memorise: say what you want in your own words. These examples show what works.

Talking and ending

You sayWhat happens
"Othos, end the call" / "hang up"Othos says goodbye and ends the call.
"Switch to code chat"Opens the typed code chat after Othos finishes speaking.

Notes

You sayWhat happens
"Remember that my sister's birthday is June 3rd."Saves a note on your computer (in agent\brain\).
"What did I tell you about my sister?"Searches your notes.

News and weather

You sayWhat happens
"What's the news?" / "keep going" / "tell me more about the second one" / "where's that from?"A news round-up, more headlines, follow-ups and sources.
"Any breaking news?" / "summarize the news"The last few hours, or a spoken summary.
"What's the local news?" / "state news"Your own feeds from personal.json.
"PC hardware news" / "tech news" / "design news"Free news feeds.
"What are people reading?" / "bestsellers"New York Times lists (needs a free New York Times key in .env).
"What's the weather like?" / "will it rain tomorrow?"Weather for your town.
"Search the web for ..."Searches the web with Tavily directly.
"What's Apple's stock price?"A quoted price with its source and time.

News and weather use free sources first. Some extras (The Guardian, the New York Times, YouTube, Ravelry, Twitch) need a free key from that service, which you paste into agent\.env. They are all optional. See Advanced.

The camera

You sayWhat happens
"Take a look" / "what am I holding?" / "read this label"Takes one picture with your webcam and describes it on your PC. It never streams video, and only looks when you ask.

Keeping out of a game's way

You sayWhat happens
"Gaming mode on" / "gaming mode off" / "is gaming mode on?"Moves Othos out of the way when a game needs your PC's power.

Asking The Smithy for code

See The Smithy for these: "Ask the Smithy to review ...", "What's the Smithy working on?", "Keep Smithy task 3".

Recording a how-to

Othos can record what you do, step by step, so you have a written guide afterwards.

You sayWhat happens
"Othos, start a how-to: setting up OBS"Othos says "Recording", then stays quiet. Each thing you say becomes a step, with a picture of the window in front.
"Scratch that" / "note: use the second port"Removes the last step / adds a tip to it.
"Take a picture" (or "snap") / "no picture"Another picture for the last step / removes its pictures.
"Pause the how-to" / "resume the how-to" / "how many steps?"While recording.
"Finish the how-to"Saves it in agent\drafts\howto\.

Pictures are taken only while you are recording, and only of the window in front, never your whole screen. Chat apps, password managers and sign-in screens are skipped. They stay on your PC.
