Words explained

Short, plain meanings for the words you will meet. You do not need to memorise any of this.

WordWhat it means
AI model (or just "model")A program that has learned to understand and write language (or look at pictures). Othos uses several small ones, each for one job: talking, searching your notes, looking through the camera, writing code. They are big files stored on your PC.
LocalHappens on your own computer. Nothing is sent over the internet.
CloudHappens on someone else's computer over the internet. In the usual Othos setup, only speech (hearing you and speaking back) uses the cloud.
OllamaA free program that downloads AI models and runs them on your PC. Othos asks Ollama to do its thinking.
Key (API key)A long code that works like a password for an online service. You get one by signing up on the service's website. Keep keys private.
DeepgramAn online service that turns your voice into text ("speech-to-text"). Used in the usual setup.
RimeAn online service that gives Othos its speaking voice ("text-to-speech"). Used in the usual setup.
Mixed modeThe usual setup: speech goes through Deepgram and Rime, everything else stays on your PC.
Fully offline modeSpeech runs on your PC too. Needs a graphics card and extra programs. See Advanced.
The call pageThe web page you talk to Othos on. It runs on your own PC at http://127.0.0.1:3000, and no one else can open it.
127.0.0.1An address that always means "this computer". Othos uses it so its pieces talk to each other without leaving your PC.
Python, Node.jsProgramming tools that Othos is built on. You install them once and never use them directly.
PowerShellA Windows window where you type instructions. You only need it for setup and for checking things.
Folder / pathA "path" is the address of a file, such as agent\.env meaning the file .env inside the folder agent.
The SmithyA background helper that writes and reviews code for you. It only makes drafts for you to read. See The Smithy.
DraftA file Othos or The Smithy made for you to read. Nothing in a draft is used, run or sent until you decide to.
TaskOne job given to The Smithy, such as "review this file".
ProviderAn online company that runs a bigger AI model for The Smithy. All are off until you turn one on. See The Smithy.
TavilyAn online search service Othos uses last, when the free sources do not have an answer.
Webcam lookOthos takes one picture and describes it on your PC, only when you ask. It never streams video.
Gaming modeA setting that moves Othos out of the way when a game needs your PC's power.
PortA numbered "door" on your PC that a program listens on. If two programs want the same door, one cannot start. See Fixing problems.
LogA text file where a program writes down what it did, handy when something goes wrong. Logs can contain what you said, so do not share them.
