Privacy

Where your information goes. In short: most things stay on your PC, a few things go to named online services, and Othos never sends, posts or deletes anything for you.

Stays on your computer

  • The AI model that does the thinking and talking.
  • Your notes and the search through them.
  • Webcam pictures. They are taken only when you ask, one at a time, and are never streamed.
  • The code chat, and The Smithy (unless you turn on an online provider).
  • Your settings: .env, personal.json, profile.md, and the logs.

What Othos will never do

  • It never sends, posts or deletes anything for you. Anything like that needs your yes, checked by the program itself.
  • The Smithy never runs, installs or applies anything. It only makes drafts.
  • It never writes to your profile.md.
  • Its call page and call server listen on your own PC only (127.0.0.1), so other devices on your network cannot reach them.

Keeping your keys safe

  • Keys in .env are like passwords. Do not post them, send them or put them on the internet. Othos's own files for them (.env, personal.json, profile.md) are kept out of Git, the tool developers use to share code.
  • Keys you add in The Smithy's Providers window are encrypted for your Windows account and stored outside the Othos folder, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Othos\keys.dat.
  • Logs (agent\logs\) can contain what you said. Do not share them without reading them first.
Advanced: how-to screenshots

While you are recording a how-to, Othos takes a picture of the window in front: one per step you say, or when you say "take a picture". There is no timer and no background capture. It skips chat apps (Signal, Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp), password managers, Windows sign-in and permission prompts, and any window whose title mentions a password, credentials, signing in or two-factor codes. They are saved in agent\drafts\howto\ and go nowhere else.
